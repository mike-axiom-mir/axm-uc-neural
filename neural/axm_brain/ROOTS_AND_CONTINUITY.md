# AXM roots and neural continuity

AXM Brain carries four roots:

1. **Truth**
2. **Agency**
3. **Continuity**
4. **Wisdom Before Speed**

They are not the software directions. CREATE / USE / PLAY / LEARN /
DISCOVER are labels for the direction of experience.

## What the roots are

The roots are an inspectable transition-review contract.

They are **not**:

- a benchmark;
- a reward to maximize;
- an obedience rule;
- a hidden control channel;
- a capability ceiling;
- a rule that exact weights must remain unchanged.

Every AXM brain snapshot includes the exact root contract and its fingerprint.
A snapshot with a different root contract is not silently accepted as the same
brain schema.

## Why Continuity sits beside plastic learning

Continual learning changes shared neural parameters. New learning can therefore
damage older demonstrated behavior even when the new behavior is useful.

AXM does not answer that by freezing the brain.

A deterministic **Continuity Spine** stores explicit behavioral probes beside
the neural state. A probe records a known input sequence and the demonstrated
output sequence from an anchored brain state.

A later candidate may change every internal weight and still pass if it
continues to demonstrate the anchored behavior within the stated tolerance.

This separates:

- **representation continuity** — not required;
- **behavioral continuity** — measurable;
- **provenance continuity** — preserved by the anchor record.

## Preserve / supersede / forget

Every continuity probe has one explicit disposition.

### PRESERVE

The capability remains part of the accepted continuity surface. A regression
holds the candidate transition.

### SUPERSEDE

The old probe remains inspectable for provenance but no longer blocks a
candidate. It must name a replacement probe and a reason.

### FORGET

The old probe remains inspectable but no longer blocks a candidate. An explicit
reason is required.

Continuity therefore means **no silent loss**, not permanent immutability.

## Transition review

A transition can be reviewed with:

```text
previous accepted brain
        |
        +---- state delta --------+
        |                         |
candidate brain              AXM root evidence
        |                         |
continuity probes                 |
        +-----------+-------------+
                    |
             ACCEPT or HOLD
```

An ACCEPT requires:

- preserved continuity probes to pass; and
- explicit PASS evidence for Truth, Agency, Continuity, and Wisdom Before
  Speed.

The gate does not edit the candidate and does not decide what the neural brain
is allowed to think. It only decides whether the caller has enough evidence to
promote that candidate as the next canonical state.

## Root meanings in this implementation

**Truth** — claims and transition decisions remain grounded in inspectable
evidence; uncertainty remains explicit.

**Agency** — capability is not silently converted into domination or obedience;
user and system choices stay explicit inside granted authority.

**Continuity** — demonstrated capability, provenance, and recoverable lineage
are preserved unless explicitly superseded or forgotten.

**Wisdom Before Speed** — reversible and evidenced change is preferred over
faster irreversible change when uncertainty is material.

## Truth boundary

Passing a continuity probe proves only that the tested behavior remained within
the probe tolerance in that tested state sequence.

It does not prove that the neural representation is unchanged, that the brain
"understands" the same concept, or that the behavior generalizes outside the
probe. Broader claims require broader behavioral and causal evidence.
